Quick capture

Share from YouTube or WhatsApp. Save to Google Sheets in one action, or keep an offline copy.

Source library

Saved captures on this phone, organised by project. Cloud-only records are available in the Google Sheets cloud library.

Google Sheets connection

Paste the same working Apps Script /exec URL you used previously. Your Google account must be authorised to use the cloud app.

Projects

The project dropdown contains your five initial projects. Add or rename projects in the Google Sheets Projects tab; the cloud library reads those directly. The phone's quick-capture dropdown can be updated in a future release.

How saving works

Save to Google Sheets opens a secure Google Apps Script submission and receipt. Tap “Return to Tackled.” after success to mark the local item as synced. If you close the receipt, the item stays marked pending and you can safely retry; duplicate submissions are ignored. This is not background sync.
